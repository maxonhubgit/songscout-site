Delete your account

In the app: Profile → scroll down → "Delete account". Everything is deleted permanently, including your songs, comments, pins and profile photo.

Report a song, comment, pin or person

Tap "…" next to the content or on the profile and choose "Report". Reports are anonymous and reviewed within 24 hours. You can also block someone there.

Notifications

Choose per group what you hear about: tap the bell next to a group on "Your Groups". Streak reminders can be turned off in your profile.

Song Map

Song Map needs precise location, because pins are only visible within 150 m. You can turn it on in iOS Settings → Apps → SongScout → Location → Precise Location.
