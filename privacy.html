2. Overview

SongScout lets groups of friends share, like, comment on and relay songs, and leave songs at places ("Song Map"). This policy covers the SongScout app and this website. There are no ads, no tracking and no analytics tools, and data is never sold.

3. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When you open a page, GitHub processes your IP address and technical request data to deliver it and to keep the service secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The website sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads nothing from third parties: fonts, images and styles come from this site itself. More information: GitHub Privacy Statement.

5. Who can see your data

  • Groups: songs, likes, reactions and comments are visible only to members of that group. Your full profile is visible only to people you share a group with.
  • Relays: when someone relays one of your songs into another group, your username is shown there as the original sharer, never the group it came from. You can turn this off in your profile ("Credit me on relays"). If either of you blocked the other, no name is shown.
  • Song Map: pinned songs are visible only to people within about 150 meters, together with your username. Pins can't be searched. People who find your pin can save it; it then stays on their "Your map", including its place.
  • Song Map profile: people who find your pin can open a small profile with your username, profile photo, Instagram username and the number of songs you pinned. Your groups and shared songs are not shown. You can turn this off in your profile ("Profile on Song Map").
  • Mystery picks: who shared an anonymous pick is shown only after people guess or it is revealed.

6. Service providers (processors)

  • Supabase (Supabase Inc., USA): database, sign-in, file storage and server functions. Data is stored in a data center in Frankfurt am Main (EU), under a data processing agreement with EU Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • OpenAI (OpenAI, L.L.C., USA): automatic checks for abusive or prohibited content. Usernames, profile photos, song titles, comments and Song Map texts are sent, without your email address. According to OpenAI, data sent through its API is not used for training. Basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; transfer to the USA under EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
  • Apple: push notifications (Apple Push Notification service) and, if you use them, Sign in with Apple, Apple Music (MusicKit) and song recognition (ShazamKit).
  • GitHub (GitHub Inc., USA): hosting of this website (see 3).
  • Music details: to show title, artist and cover art, our servers look up the shared song on Apple (iTunes) and, for SoundCloud links, on SoundCloud. No user data is sent. When you pick the matching song while sharing, the app searches Apple's catalog directly from your device; Apple receives the search term and your IP address.

7. Music services and Instagram

When you open a song you switch to the Spotify, Apple Music or SoundCloud app or website, where their privacy policies apply. The 30-second preview is loaded directly from Apple, which sees your IP address. If you choose Apple Music as your listening app, the app can, with your permission, add songs and playlists to your Apple Music library; it does not read your library. When you tap an Instagram username, the Instagram app or website opens, where Instagram's privacy policy applies.

8. Retention and deletion

  • We keep your data as long as your account exists.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time (Profile → "Delete account"). This permanently deletes your profile, profile photo, songs, likes, reactions, comments, pins, saved pins, group memberships and push tokens, and revokes your Sign in with Apple. Songs other people relayed stay, but no longer refer to you.
  • Reports are kept as long as needed to handle them and to prevent abuse.
  • Server logs are deleted automatically by our hosting providers after a short time.

9. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). You can withdraw consents (location, microphone, notifications) at any time in iOS Settings. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, for example the Bavarian Data Protection Authority (BayLDA). For any request, an email to the address above is enough.
